05 — Good to know

Questions,
answered.

What can I build?

Anything connected to vision, colour, optics, accessibility, perception, or the technology that helps us experience the world differently. A tiny colour tool is just as welcome as smart glasses.

How do I earn RGB?

Ship a project with a public link, tell us how many hours you spent, and submit it for review. Every approved hour is worth 50 RGB. RGB stacks across every project you make, and 10 RGB equals $1.

Can I build more than one project?

Yes. Start small, experiment, and keep building. Each project gets its own review and contributes to your balance.

What makes a good submission?

A working project, a clear description of what you made, and a link that reviewers can open. Be honest about your hours and tell us what you learned.

When do I receive my grant?

At the end of the event, you can spend your RGB in the SEE YA Shop. Grant requests are reviewed and fulfilled by the event team.
